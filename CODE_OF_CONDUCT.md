# Code of Conduct

StatQED is committed to a professional, rigorous, and inclusive research environment.

Participants must:

- critique technical claims rather than people;
- disclose uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- preserve attribution;
- welcome corrections and counterexamples;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and retaliation;
- keep private vulnerability and conduct reports confidential;
- distinguish scientific disagreement from misconduct.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner. Enforcement procedures and a broader committee will be established before open community governance.
