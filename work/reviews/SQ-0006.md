### Field semantics and formal boundary — APPROVE

The object has exactly six fields. `analysis_id` is an opaque ASCII fixture
identifier, `probability_context = not_applicable` introduces no probability
statement, and `features = []` defines no feature-element ontology. CDDL owns
structure; the standard-library semantic validator owns ASCII/length and field
meaning. Profile decoding, deterministic bytes, CDDL shape, schema semantics,
and fixture digest are separate result layers. No data, statistical,
logical-data, artifact, theorem, certificate, provenance, or interpretation
semantics were introduced.

### Independent validation, goldens, and digest frames — APPROVE

The retained corpus contains 5 accepted fixtures, 85 rejected cases, and 3
detected deliberate divergences. Both frozen SQ-0005 lineages and a direct
standards recipe agree on accepted canonical bytes. The conformance reviewer
