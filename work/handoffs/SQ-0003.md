## Update, rollback, and generated-file policy

For an update, research official sources, treat Lean/Mathlib as one exact pair,
change the toolchain/config/generated manifest together, regenerate the actual
report, repeat normal and isolated source builds, run every mutation and hosted
gate, and obtain new independent reviews. A pin change cannot be justified by
mere local compilation.

Rollback restores `lean-toolchain`, `lakefile.toml`, the Lake-generated
manifest, and matching generated report as one reviewed set. Partial rollback
must fail. Lake alone generates `lake-manifest.json`; the reviewed helper alone
generates `Reports/axioms.json`. `.lake`, downloaded packages, Python caches,
and temporary worktrees are not committed.
