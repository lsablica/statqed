# Planned Plan 0004: Conformal and Sequential Inference

Backlog: SQ-0048–SQ-0054.

## Goal

Support one conformal prediction family and one e-value/confidence-sequence family with finite-sample or anytime-valid guarantees.

## Required work

- exchangeability and filtration/stopping semantics;
- rank/tie conventions and finite-sample coverage;
- black-box predictor boundary;
- e-process/test-martingale definitions;
- Ville-style guarantee and confidence-sequence inversion;
- rank/product witnesses and exact/interval arithmetic;
- distinction among data, training randomness, algorithm randomness, and sequential observation;
- frontends and publication exemplar.
