# Statistical Assurance Graph Specification

Status: **Draft; taxonomy blocked on RFC-0002 and RFC-0004**.

The assurance graph is a typed DAG.

## Initial node kinds

- claim;
- theorem application;
- numeric fact;
- data-derived fact;
- transformation;
- external assumption;
- attestation;
- diagnostic;
- citation;
- provenance entity/activity/agent;
- unresolved obligation.

## Edge kinds

- deductive dependency;
- checker establishes fact;
- theorem instantiation;
- derived from data;
- records attestation of premise declaration;
- diagnostic informs judgment;
- generated by activity;
- attributed to source;
- incompatible with;
- deductively refines or weakens under a proof lock;
- review-classifies relation (non-deductive).

The type system forbids diagnostic, attestation, citation, provenance, and policy records from discharging an external assumption. A registered theorem may derive a distinct proposition only from all explicit premises. Cycles are invalid. Every reportable conclusion resolves to its dependency closure and exposes unresolved/external leaves.
