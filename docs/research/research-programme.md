# Research Programme

StatQED is intended to generate research, not merely translations of known results.

Primary programmes:

1. formal semantics of statistical analyses and assurance graphs;
2. formal applied-statistics libraries and theorem interfaces;
3. solver-independent numerical certificates;
4. cross-language statistical semantic equivalence;
5. assumption minimization and identification calculus;
6. formalization-driven counterexamples, corrections, and explicit constants;
7. source-faithful AI formalization and review workflows;
8. proof-carrying artifacts, archival verification, and provenance;
9. benchmarks for statistical reasoning and overclaim detection;
10. privacy-preserving and succinct verification for large/private analyses.

Each programme should publish datasets, theorem/source maps, failures, and reproducible artifacts.
