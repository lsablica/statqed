# Method-Pack Contract

A method pack is the unit of scientific extension.

## Required components

1. scientific scope, estimands, procedures, claims, and non-goals;
2. primary source corpus and source audits;
3. formal definitions and frozen theorem signatures;
4. external assumptions and randomness scopes;
5. numerical obligation and witness type;
6. checker plus checker-soundness theorem;
7. at least one untrusted producer and independent oracle;
8. IR and artifact schema extensions;
9. frontend constructors/adapters;
10. positive, boundary, malformed, corruption, ablation, and overclaim tests;
11. theorem registry, citations, trust report, and maturity record;
12. performance and resource limits.

## Maturity

- Draft: design/source work incomplete.
- Experimental: end-to-end implementation exists but interfaces may change.
- Candidate: semantics and statement locks frozen for independent review.
- Stable: versioned, reviewed, conformance-tested, migrated, and archived.

A theorem file alone is not a method pack.
