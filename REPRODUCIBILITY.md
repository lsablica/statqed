# Reproducibility and Archival Policy

StatQED distinguishes replay, recomputation, independent checking, and kernel verification.

Every executable release will provide:

- source and dependency locks;
- exact toolchain versions;
- build/test commands;
- schema, theorem, and method-pack content hashes;
- canonical example artifacts and expected digests;
- offline verification instructions;
- provenance and citation metadata;
- archived verifier sources/binaries where policy permits;
- known limitations and platform coverage.

A random seed supports replay but does not establish algorithm convergence, statistical validity, or source-data truth. Historical artifacts remain verifiable through archived locks or explicit reviewed migrations.
